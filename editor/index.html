Pick the Town tool (T), then click for the radius above, or press and drag out to size it ([ ] resize). Reroll lays the last one out again.

Underground

Pick the Underground tool (U) and drag a rectangle over the void: it carves the cave, mine or crypt there. Rock walls rise around cave floor and stone walls around dungeon floor when it's built; paint Cave floor, Dungeon floor or Void to reshape it by hand. Start a New map as Underground for a dungeon facet.

Places

Type a new name and press Enter. A town's region, and signs with the old name nearby, follow the new name. Go shows the place.

For a dungeon facet: a facet of its own that holds every cave, mine and crypt, reached by teleporters from the overworld. Players never see the difference.

Plates give the land its shape and push up mountain ranges where they meet; wind from the west brings rain to the coasts and leaves dry land behind the mountains; rivers run down to the sea, big ones through deltas, and every lake spills out through a river. Roads between towns follow the valleys and cross rivers on bridges. The same seed and settings give the same world.
