Underground

Pick the Underground tool (U) and drag a rectangle over the void: it carves the cave, mine or crypt there. Rock walls rise around cave floor and stone walls around dungeon floor when it's built; paint Cave floor, Dungeon floor or Void to reshape it by hand. Start a New map as Underground for a dungeon facet.
