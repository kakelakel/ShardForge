ShardForge Walk walk and decorate your world at ground level

Walk the world you built

ShardForge Walk draws a map with your own client's tile art, lets you walk it with UO-like movement, and place or remove statics where you stand.

  1. Open client folder and pick your Ultima Online folder (for example C:\Games\Electronic Arts\Ultima Online Classic). Built maps in subfolders, like ShardForge-test, are found too.
  2. Choose a map in the list and press Load map.

Files are read in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Only the parts needed are read: the art file, tiledata and one map.

Needs: artLegacyMUL.uop (or art.mul + artidx.mul), tiledata.mul, animdata.mul (for animations), and map#LegacyMUL.uop or map#.mul with staidx#.mul + statics#.mul.

Movement

Walk follows UO rules: water, mountain rock, trees and walls block you, and you climb only gentle slopes. Fly goes anywhere; click a tile to jump there.
